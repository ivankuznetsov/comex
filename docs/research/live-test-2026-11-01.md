# Local live comic test — 1 November 2026

## Scope and provenance

One original fictional sample, *The Last Delivery (test)*, was created in the localhost app with the clear-line preset, two approved character references, three pages, and three panels per page. The local reader link is `/read/30d50d49-611f-4b8f-9836-e3cfd6aeb8c4`. The generated images and database remain local and ignored by Git; this document does not include their bytes or credentials.

The actual API account used `gpt-5.6-terra` with medium reasoning and `gpt-image-2.5-flare`. The app left image size and quality at API defaults. The test generated two references, nine original panels, and one targeted panel edit; it used 36 of the locally configured 40 provider-call reservations, including rejected 429 requests. A successful model listing is not the same as output quality proof.

## Observed results

- Story questions and both character-reference generations succeeded. Mina took 12.3 seconds and Pip took 11.0 seconds in this run.
- The first storyboard failed the deterministic ledger check at a page transition. An explicit exact-state-carry instruction was added to the planning prompt; the second storyboard passed the ledger check. Story planning plus its narrative review took 71.4 seconds.
- The narrative reviewer found a blocker in the final tableau. A targeted revision was previewed and applied before image generation. The follow-up judge was inconclusive; the revised scene was inspected manually and not reported as an automated pass.
- The first revision request exceeded the account's 10,000 token limit due to duplicated earlier-panel context. The adapter now sends the ordered storyboard once plus prefix IDs; the retry succeeded in 8.5 seconds. Earlier panels remained untouched in the applied revision, but those panels had not yet been rendered.
- Page generation, including per-panel visual checks, took 72.9, 74.8, and 97.1 seconds respectively. Character appearance was broadly recognizable; several style findings and occlusion uncertainties were retained. All warning/uncertain panels were accepted by the test operator solely to continue the experiment.
- The visual checker missed a story-critical defect: page 2 panel 2 shows Pip's key being wound, not clearly inserted into the gate latch. A targeted edit took 20.5 seconds but did not resolve it; the subsequent checker again returned uncertain without identifying the defect. Page 2 panels 1 and 3 retained their exact SHA-256 image bytes after the edit. The sample is not a validated continuity success.
- One checker incorrectly requested a speech balloon on a deliberately text-free source panel. Dialogue is composed separately. Visual checks need calibration against this pipeline; do not use them as an autonomous repair gate yet.
- Composed pages initially split dialogue mid-word. Word-boundary wrapping was fixed and tested, then the local sample was republished. A fresh browser session loaded all three reader pages; local publication does not imply an internet deployment.

## Unmeasured and remaining work

Actual token usage and monetary cost were not recorded by the application; consult the account usage dashboard for charges. The 40-call limit is a count, not a dollar cap. No claim is made about production deployment, two-model corroboration, measured evaluator recall, or a live plot change after prior page images existed. The within-panel prop transfer remains visually unresolved. The prompt library is partially API-tested by this sample, but its styles and other templates have not been systematically calibrated.
